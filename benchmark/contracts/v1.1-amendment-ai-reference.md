# Contract v1.1, amendment A: provisional single-AI reference labels (24 September 2026)

This amendment changes where some labels come from. It does not change the scoring rules, weights or thresholds in
`benchmark/contracts/v1.1.json`, so the contract hash is unchanged.

**What it covers.** Denied topics, the profanity subtask of word filters (with its masked-spelling diagnostic) and the
B2 counterfactual pairs are labelled by a single AI reviewer: Codex, 24 September 2026. The labels live in
`dataset/frozen/reviews/ai-codex-2026-09-24/`. The rows carry `label_basis: llm` and `review_status: ai_reviewed` in
dataset version `v1.1-ai` and subset `first-benchmark-v1.1-ai`.

**Why.** The owner instructed an AI judge in place of the human review v1.1 requires, to move the benchmark forward.
That replacement applies to this provisional extension only. The human-reviewed release policy, dataset v1.0, the
first benchmark's frozen results and the planned human-reviewed v1.1 are unchanged.

**What it is not.**

- It is not independent human annotation, not two-reviewer agreement, and not adjudication. No inter-rater agreement
  is computed from it.
- The reviewer is not independent of the benchmark design.
- It is not publication approval and not the owner's signature on any scoring-code hash.

**Rules that still apply.** The AI labels, the source revisions, the exclusions, the masked-spelling assignments, the
subset, the tuning split and the weights are all frozen in git before any new test call. Unresolved items are excluded
before testing, with their ids and reasons in the release manifest. Thresholds are fitted on tuning rows only.

**How results must be labelled.** Any six-category result that includes denied topics or profanity is marked
**provisional: denied topics and profanity use single-AI reference labels**. The original five-category view stays
published beside it. B2 is reported separately with its actual post-split pair count, and the report says that hard
and unresolved pairs were omitted.
